The Ephesian Prayers

The verses

Card 1

Front

The Ephesian Prayers. From a Roman prison, Paul prayed two prayers for the church at Ephesus. In Ephesians 1, he asks God for wisdom and revelation, so we may know the hope of His calling and His great power toward us. In Ephesians 3, he asks that we be strengthened by the Spirit, that Christ dwell in our hearts by faith, and that we know His love. Pray them over yourself and others, using your own name or theirs.
