About this set

The verse selection and exact prayer come only from Peace With God’s four-step page, in its displayed order. Each Scripture side contains one complete King James Version verse; the source page uses modern-English wording and excerpts. The prayer is source text, not Scripture. No endorsement is implied.

Cards are 86 × 54 mm. Scripture is 16.32 pt and the prayer is 15.12 pt at 600 DPI. Card 7 has an intentionally blank back. The complete 80-word prayer is on both sides of card 8. Print the review PDF at Actual Size / 100%; it is not duplex-imposed. The ZIP includes individual black-and-white PNGs, covers, outlined cover SVGs, editable text and source notes.
