Make Your Own Scripture Wallet

Standard set · 40 cards · KJV · printable PNGs

You do not need aluminum blanks or a laser engraver. You can print these cards on blank printable business-card sheets (for example Avery business card sheets) or on cardstock and cut them to size. The standard card size is 86 × 54 mm (about credit-card / business-card size). Each PNG is 1015 × 637 pixels at 300 DPI.

How the system works

  1. Print the front cover, the back plate, and the 40 scripture cards (front and back of each card).
  2. Cut to 86 × 54 mm if your sheet does not already perforate to that size.
  3. Stack the cards in a small wallet, sleeve, or banded set with the cover and back plate.
  4. Carry it. Read a card daily. When you have memorized one side, flip the card and work on the other side.
  5. The goal is simple: meditate on and memorize the Word of God.

Print on blank business cards or cardstock

Aluminum laser engraving is optional. For most people the easiest path is:

  • Buy blank printable business card sheets (Avery and similar brands sell sheets sized for business cards), or
  • Print on sturdy cardstock and cut to 86 × 54 mm.

Scale each PNG to the physical card size when printing (the files are already 1015 × 637 px at 300 DPI for 86 × 54 mm).

Using AI to make cards in another translation

If you want a personal set in a different English translation, you can ask an AI image or design tool (ChatGPT, Grok, Gemini, and others) to create card PNGs. Please respect the copyright of modern translations: many are not public domain. Use them only in ways their license allows (often personal use). The KJV set on this site is free to print and share because the KJV is public domain in most of the world.

Sample prompt you can copy and adapt:

Please create a Scripture wallet card PNG image with these exact specs: - Size: 1015 × 637 pixels (86 × 54 mm at 300 DPI) - Background: plain white - Content: the Bible verse text plus the verse reference centered on the card - Font: large, bold, highly readable sans-serif (similar to DejaVu Sans Bold) - Layout: verse text in the upper/middle area; reference near the bottom - Important: keep generous margins so text does not run off the edges - One verse (or short passage) per side; I will ask for front and back separately Verse reference: [e.g. John 3:16] Translation: [name the translation] Exact verse text to use: "[paste the exact wording from that translation]" Generate a clean print-ready PNG.

Meditate Day & Night itself only gives away and hosts the King James Version. The AI tip above is so you can make a personal set if you prefer another translation — not a change in what this ministry distributes.
