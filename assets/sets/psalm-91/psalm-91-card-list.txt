Card 1 Front	Psalm 91:1	He that dwelleth in the secret place of the most High shall abide under the shadow of the Almighty.
Card 1 Back	Psalm 91:2	I will say of the LORD, He is my refuge and my fortress: my God; in him will I trust.
Card 2 Front	Psalm 91:3	Surely he shall deliver thee from the snare of the fowler, and from the noisome pestilence.
Card 2 Back	Psalm 91:4	He shall cover thee with his feathers, and under his wings shalt thou trust: his truth shall be thy shield and buckler.
Card 3 Front	Psalm 91:5	Thou shalt not be afraid for the terror by night; nor for the arrow that flieth by day;
Card 3 Back	Psalm 91:6	Nor for the pestilence that walketh in darkness; nor for the destruction that wasteth at noonday.
Card 4 Front	Psalm 91:7	A thousand shall fall at thy side, and ten thousand at thy right hand; but it shall not come nigh thee.
Card 4 Back	Psalm 91:8	Only with thine eyes shalt thou behold and see the reward of the wicked.
Card 5 Front	Psalm 91:9	Because thou hast made the LORD, which is my refuge, even the most High, thy habitation;
Card 5 Back	Psalm 91:10	There shall no evil befall thee, neither shall any plague come nigh thy dwelling.
Card 6 Front	Psalm 91:11	For he shall give his angels charge over thee, to keep thee in all thy ways.
Card 6 Back	Psalm 91:12	They shall bear thee up in their hands, lest thou dash thy foot against a stone.
Card 7 Front	Psalm 91:13	Thou shalt tread upon the lion and adder: the young lion and the dragon shalt thou trample under feet.
Card 7 Back	Psalm 91:14	Because he hath set his love upon me, therefore will I deliver him: I will set him on high, because he hath known my name.
Card 8 Front	Psalm 91:15	He shall call upon me, and I will answer him: I will be with him in trouble; I will deliver him, and honour him.
Card 8 Back	Psalm 91:16	With long life will I satisfy him, and shew him my salvation.
