Why we only give away the King James Version

Meditate Day & Night gives away Scripture Wallets printed with the King James Version (KJV) only.

Accuracy and faithfulness from the Received Text

The KJV was translated from the traditional Hebrew Masoretic Text and the Greek Textus Receptus (Received Text) tradition that the church long used. We value that textual foundation and the careful, reverent work of the translators who sought to put God’s Word into English with faithfulness and gravity.

Timeless, memorable wording

The cadence and vocabulary of the KJV have helped generations memorize Scripture. Phrases such as “meditate day and night,” “every word that proceedeth out of the mouth of God,” and “Thy word have I hid in mine heart” stay with people. For a wallet meant to be carried and memorized, memorable wording matters.

Consistency

Using one standard text keeps every card, cover, and page on MeditateDayNight.com consistent. When someone receives a wallet from us, they know the verses match the KJV they can look up and share with others without confusion about which wording we used.

Free to print and share

The King James Version is in the public domain in most of the world. That means we can print, engrave, and give away these cards freely, and you can download the PNGs and print your own without a translation license. Other modern translations are often copyrighted; if you make cards in another translation for personal use, please respect that translation’s copyright terms (see the sample AI prompt on the Make Your Own page).
