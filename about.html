Our Mission

The main goal of Meditate Day & Night is simple: getting the Word of God out.

“But his delight is in the law of the LORD; and in his law doth he meditate day and night.”Psalm 1:2 (KJV)

We make Scripture Wallets that put the King James Bible's words in people's pockets, purses, and bags. Right now every Scripture Wallet is given away free. If you would like one for yourself, your church, or someone who needs encouragement, just ask.

“So then faith cometh by hearing, and hearing by the word of God.”Romans 10:17 (KJV)
“For God so loved the world, that he gave his only begotten Son, that whosoever believeth in him should not perish, but have everlasting life.”John 3:16 (KJV)
