Custom Orders

Scripture Cards made for your church, ministry, or business, with your own cover, your own list of verses, or both.

Custom cover

Your logo and name at the top, your website below it, and the set title at the bottom, engraved on the front cover of any Scripture Card set. The back of the cover carries a small Meditate Day & Night logo.

Custom scripture list

Choose the verses you want your people to carry, for a sermon series, a class, a retreat, or an outreach. We engrave one verse per side, word for word, with a cover title you choose.

Both together

Put your logo and name on the cover of your own scripture list, or on any of our Scripture Card sets.

Bible translations

We make Scripture Cards in the King James Version (KJV), the Spanish Reina-Valera 1909, and other languages that have a public-domain translation of the Bible, such as the Burmese Judson Bible. If you need a language not listed here, ask us and we will check for a public-domain translation. Why we use the KJV.
